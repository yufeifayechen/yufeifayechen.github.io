04 · About

Reporting across languages and financial systems.

我关注资本、监管、技术与企业战略如何塑造公司、市场与普通人的选择。
I report on how capital, regulation, technology, and corporate strategy shape companies, markets, and everyday decisions.

At Caixin, an investigation into a struggling state-owned enterprise taught me to look beyond official narratives. By reconciling regulatory filings, internal documents, and conflicting records, I traced how weak oversight, failed trade-financing arrangements, and unsuccessful investments contributed to the company’s near-collapse.

Work in Chinese newsrooms and financial institutions has shown me both how market narratives are produced and how reporters can test them. At NYU, I am expanding that foundation through business reporting, finance, economics, accounting, and data, with the goal of producing rigorous, accessible journalism for global audiences.
